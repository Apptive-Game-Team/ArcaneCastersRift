ART STYLE — faceted low-poly papercraft, matching the attached style reference image exactly: every form is built from flat polygonal planes, one flat matte color per plane, crisp creases between planes, warm light from the upper left. No texture, no grain, no noise, no gradient inside a plane, no gloss, no painterly brushwork, no realistic fur or skin. If it could be mistaken for a smooth 3D render or a digital painting, it is wrong.
