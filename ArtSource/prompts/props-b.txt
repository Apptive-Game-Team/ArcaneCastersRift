Environment prop sheet for a top-down action RPG forest research camp and ruins, square 1024x1024 canvas.
The ENTIRE background is one perfectly flat solid color: pure magenta, RGB(255,0,255), hex #FF00FF. No gradient, no texture, no floor, no ground shadow, no vignette, no border lines, no grid lines. Do NOT use magenta, pink or purple anywhere on the subjects.
CAMERA: classic top-down action RPG three-quarter view. The camera looks down at about 45 degrees: we see the top of the head and shoulders and the front of the body, and the ground plane is foreshortened. Every figure stands upright with both feet visible and touching an invisible ground line.
LAYOUT: an invisible 3x3 grid of cells (about 341x341 pixels each). One object per cell, centered, resting on the bottom of its cell area, with at least 25 pixels of empty background between each object and its cell borders. Objects never touch or cross cell borders. All objects drawn at one consistent world scale (a person would be about 300 pixels tall at this scale), shrunk only if needed to fit the cell.

ART STYLE (must match exactly across the whole game): hand-painted 2D fantasy RPG game art. Clean dark brown ink outline of even weight around every shape. Cel shading with two or three flat value bands per material, plus a light painterly texture inside the shapes. Warm sunlight from the upper left, so lit faces point up-left and shadow sides face down-right. Natural, saturated forest palette. Shapes must stay readable when shrunk to 100 pixels tall. Not pixel art, not 3D render, not photo, not chibi.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no UI, no frame, no watermark, no cast shadow on the ground (the game draws its own shadow).
