Character animation sheet for a 2D game, square 1024x1024 canvas.
ATTACHED IMAGES: the style reference (a lineup of faceted creatures) defines the rendering technique; the other images define identity. Do not copy their backgrounds, layout or text.
The ENTIRE background is one perfectly flat solid color: pure magenta, RGB(255,0,255), hex #FF00FF. No gradient, no texture, no floor, no ground shadow, no vignette, no border lines, no grid lines. Do NOT use magenta, pink or purple anywhere on the subjects.
CHARACTER — Mira, an adult magic research student. EXACTLY the same person as in the attached character references (the full-body sheet and the portrait): long brown hair with long front side locks, large simple dark oval eyes, a blue hooded cape, a light grey robe with beige cuffs, a gold diamond clasp at the collar and a gold diamond belt buckle on a brown belt, a brown leather card pouch on the hip, and a wooden staff whose curled ring head holds a blue crystal. Keep the reference's face, proportions and colors. Do not add or remove clothing items. HOOD RULE: the hood of the blue cape is always DOWN, lying folded on the shoulders behind the neck. The top of the head and the long brown hair are fully visible in every pose. Never draw the hood over the head.
CAMERA: classic top-down action RPG three-quarter view. The camera looks down at about 40 degrees: we see the top of the head and shoulders and the front of the body, and the ground plane is foreshortened. Every figure stands upright with its feet touching an invisible ground line. In ALL FOUR poses Mira faces UP, away from the viewer: we see the back of the long brown hair, the hood and the back of the blue cape, and the staff. The face is not visible. The first attached image shows the same character from the front; match it exactly.
LAYOUT: an invisible 2x2 grid of 512x512 cells. One full-body figure per cell, centered horizontally, the soles at the same height in every cell (about 60 pixels above the bottom of the cell), the SAME size in every cell (figure about 360 pixels tall including the staff head), with at least 50 pixels of empty background on every side of each figure. Figures, staff and glow must never touch or cross the cell borders. Nothing else in the image.
Top-left: idle stance seen from behind, staff upright in the right hand.
Top-right: walking away from the viewer, left foot forward.
Bottom-left: walking away from the viewer, right foot forward.
Bottom-right: casting away from the viewer, the left arm raised forward holding one glowing orange rune card above shoulder height; keep the glow small and inside the cell.
ART STYLE — faceted low-poly papercraft, matching the attached style reference image exactly: every form is built from flat polygonal planes, one flat matte color per plane, crisp creases between planes, warm light from the upper left. A whole forearm is five or six planes; a head is about twenty planes. Simple solid dark oval eyes. Thin, restrained dark edge lines. No texture, no grain, no noise, no gradient inside a plane, no gloss, no painterly brushwork, no realistic fur or skin. If it could be mistaken for a smooth 3D render or a digital painting, it is wrong.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no UI, no frame, no watermark, no cast shadow on the ground (the game draws its own shadow).
