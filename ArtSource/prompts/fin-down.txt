Character animation sheet for a 2D game, square 1024x1024 canvas.
ATTACHED IMAGES: the style reference (a lineup of faceted creatures) defines the rendering technique; the other images define identity. Do not copy their backgrounds, layout or text.
The ENTIRE background is one perfectly flat solid color: pure magenta, RGB(255,0,255), hex #FF00FF. No gradient, no texture, no floor, no ground shadow, no vignette, no border lines, no grid lines. Do NOT use magenta, pink or purple anywhere on the subjects.
CHARACTER — Fin, an adult magic research student and Mira's friend. EXACTLY the person in the attached character reference: short messy brown hair with one small braided side lock ending in a white stone bead, warm brown eyes, a muted green hooded cape with a pale triangle-pattern trim, a cream-white robe with a triangle-pattern hem, a red sash belt, a brown leather book satchel on a shoulder strap, a round carved stone clasp on the chest, dark trousers, sturdy brown boots, and a gnarled forked wooden staff holding a pale yellow crystal. HOOD RULE: the hood is always DOWN behind the neck; the hair is fully visible. Keep the reference's colors and items; do not add or remove clothing items. Draw Fin in the faceted papercraft technique of the style reference, NOT in the reference's ink-comic technique.
CAMERA: classic top-down action RPG three-quarter view. The camera looks down at about 40 degrees: we see the top of the head and shoulders and the front of the body, and the ground plane is foreshortened. Every figure stands upright with its feet touching an invisible ground line. In ALL FOUR poses Fin faces DOWN toward the viewer.
LAYOUT: an invisible 2x2 grid of 512x512 cells. One full-body figure per cell, centered horizontally, the soles at the same height in every cell (about 60 pixels above the bottom of the cell), the SAME size in every cell (figure about 360 pixels tall including the staff head), with at least 50 pixels of empty background on every side of each figure. Figures, staff and glow must never touch or cross the cell borders. Nothing else in the image.
Top-left: idle stance, staff upright in the right hand, left hand relaxed, a friendly grin.
Top-right: walking toward the viewer, left foot forward, right foot back.
Bottom-left: walking toward the viewer, right foot forward, left foot back.
Bottom-right: casting, staff in the right hand, the left arm thrust forward holding one glowing orange rune card; keep the glow small and close to the card.
ART STYLE — faceted low-poly papercraft, matching the attached style reference image exactly: every form is built from flat polygonal planes, one flat matte color per plane, crisp creases between planes, warm light from the upper left. A whole forearm is five or six planes; a head is about twenty planes. Simple solid dark oval eyes. Thin, restrained dark edge lines.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no UI, no frame, no watermark, no cast shadow on the ground (the game draws its own shadow).
